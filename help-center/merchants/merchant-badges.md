# Merchant badges

Some merchants on HashLiquid P2P have a badge next to their name. Each badge shows a status that HashLiquid has given to that merchant.

## Badges at a glance

| Badge | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| [**Partner Merchant**](#partner-merchant) | Founding merchants committed to competitive prices, fast trades and reliable order completion in your market. |

## Partner Merchant

Founding merchants committed to competitive prices, fast trades and reliable order completion in your market.

A Partner Merchant is a dedicated merchant ready to take your order.

### Where you'll see the badge

The Partner Merchant badge appears directly after the merchant's name:

* On the P2P ad list, on both the **Buy** and **Sell** tabs
* On the merchant's profile page

On the website, hover over or click the badge to see what it means. In the app, tap the badge.

### How to become a Partner Merchant

Partner Merchant is a status HashLiquid gives to selected merchants after a manual review. There is no application form for it in the app.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
**Become a HashLiquid Merchant**

You must already be an approved Merchant on HashLiquid. If you aren't one yet, apply first.

Full guide: [How do I apply to become a HashLiquid Merchant?](become-a-merchant.md)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Have a strong merchant track record**

You need a good merchant profile on other exchanges.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Contact the BD team on Telegram**

Message the HashLiquid BD team at [t.me/fastbuild01](https://t.me/fastbuild01) to apply. Only use this official link.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Get vetted and approved**

The BD team reviews and vets each application manually. Once you're approved, the badge appears on your ads and profile automatically. Nothing changes in how you post ads.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
HashLiquid manages Partner Merchant status and can remove it at any time. When it's removed, the badge no longer appears.
{% endhint %}
