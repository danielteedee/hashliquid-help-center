# Glossary

## Trading

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Advertisement** | An offer posted by a Merchant, showing the price, order limits, Payment Methods, fee, and Terms & Remarks. |
| **Merchant** | An approved trader who posts Advertisements. You trade with a Merchant when you place an order. |
| **Express** | Finds an Advertisement for you based on the amount and Payment Method you choose. |
| **P2P Zone** | Lets you browse Advertisements and pick one yourself. |
| **Maker / Taker order** | A Maker order comes from your own Advertisement. A Taker order is placed on someone else's Advertisement. |
| **Terms & Remarks** | The Merchant's own conditions for an Advertisement. Always read them before placing an order. |
| **Counterparty** | The other person in your order: the seller when you buy, or the buyer when you sell. |
| **Order chat** | The chat inside each order. Keep all order communication here. |
| **I've Paid** | The button a buyer selects after sending payment. |
| **Release Crypto** | The button a seller selects to send the crypto to the buyer after confirming payment. |
| **Dispute** | A request for HashLiquid Support to review an order the buyer and seller can't resolve in the order chat. |

## Order statuses

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending Payment** | Waiting for the buyer to pay. |
| **Pending Release** | The buyer has selected I've Paid. Waiting for the seller to confirm payment and release the crypto. |
| **Releasing** | The crypto is being released. |
| **Completed** | The order is finished. |
| **Canceled** | The order was canceled. |
| **In Dispute** | HashLiquid Support is reviewing the order. The crypto stays frozen until the Dispute is resolved. |

## Wallets and transfers

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **HashLiquid Account** | Your USDT balance on HashLiquid. In the app it may appear as Exchange Wallet or HashLiquid Wallet. |
| **Connected Wallet** | A wallet you connected with Binance Wallet or WalletConnect. |
| **Binance Funding Wallet** | Your Binance funding balance, shown when you access HashLiquid with Binance Login. |
| **USDT** | The stablecoin you deposit, withdraw, trade, and receive rewards in on HashLiquid. |
| **BNB Smart Chain (BSC)** | The blockchain network HashLiquid uses for USDT deposits, withdrawals, and reward payments. USDT on it uses the BEP20 standard. |
| **Network Fee** | The blockchain fee for a withdrawal, shown before you confirm. |
| **Receive Amount** | What arrives after the Network Fee is taken off. |
| **Transaction hash (TXID)** | The unique ID of an on-chain transfer. Share it with HashLiquid Support if a transfer is delayed. |

## Account

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **KYC** | Identity verification. You complete it from Verifications in your account menu. |
| **Verified identity** | Your identity as confirmed by KYC. Accounts linked to the same verified identity are treated as one for reward limits. |
| **Payment Method** | A bank or payment account in your own name that buyers pay into when you sell. |
| **Liveness check** | A short camera check during verification to confirm you are a real person. |
| **HashLiquid Support** | HashLiquid's support team, reached through the headset icon. The chat window is labeled Hash Support. |

## Rewards

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Reward Center** | Where you track reward tasks, referrals, Credit, and payouts. |
| **My Tasks** | Shows your first-purchase task and new-user reward progress. |
| **My Referrals** | Shows your referral link, referred users, rewards, and X/50 progress. |
| **Credit** | A recorded reward that hasn't been paid yet. It can't be traded, transferred, or withdrawn. |
| **Payout History** | Paid rewards, with amounts and TXIDs. |
| **Settlement list** | The list of approved rewards prepared for payment. Your receiving address must be saved before it is generated. |
| **Eligible purchase** | A single P2P Buy order of at least 30 USDT that completes successfully and passes review. |

## Merchants

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **P2P Merchant Application Form** | The form you complete to apply to become a Merchant. Open it with Post Ads. |
| **CIPHER** | The tool approved Merchants use to manage Advertisements, orders, Disputes, and their Merchant Funding Account. |
| **Merchant Funding Account** | A Merchant's funding balance, managed in CIPHER. |
