# What should I do if there is a payment or crypto-release problem?

If a P2P order does not proceed as expected, keep the order open and contact the counterparty through the order chat. Do not move the conversation outside HashLiquid.

## I paid, but the seller hasn't released the crypto

1. Confirm that the payment was completed successfully and was sent to the account shown in the order.
2. Upload valid payment proof if requested.
3. Contact the seller through the order chat.
4. If the issue is not resolved and **Dispute** is available, [raise a Dispute](disputes.md) from the order page.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Do not cancel an order after you have paid unless the funds have been returned to your payment account.
{% endhint %}

## The buyer marked the order as paid, but I haven't received payment

Check the receiving account directly. Do not rely only on a screenshot, text message, or payment notification supplied by the buyer.

If the full payment has not arrived:

1. Do not release the crypto.
2. Ask the buyer to verify the payment through the order chat.
3. [Raise a Dispute](disputes.md) when the option becomes available if the issue remains unresolved.

## I released the crypto but haven't received payment

Check the receiving account and payment details again, then [raise a Dispute](disputes.md) immediately. Provide the relevant account transaction record and order information.

## I paid the wrong account or entered the wrong amount

Contact the counterparty in the order chat as soon as possible. Do not send another payment unless both parties have confirmed how the first payment will be handled. [Raise a Dispute](disputes.md) if you cannot resolve the issue.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Never release crypto because the counterparty is pressuring you. Release it only after confirming that the full payment has reached your own account.
{% endhint %}
