# Campaign Rewards at a Glance

{% hint style="warning" %}
**The campaign is temporarily on hold.** New participation is suspended while we complete reviews and verification. The revised rewards below apply when the campaign resumes. [Read the latest update](campaign-pause.md)
{% endhint %}

The rewards for the New User Trading Campaign (Trade, Invite and Earn) have been revised. Here is everything you need to know in one place.

## Reward amounts

| Activity | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| **First purchase:** complete your first P2P Buy order of at least 30 USDT | **1 USDT**, one time |
| **User referral:** invite a friend who completes their first eligible purchase | **1 USDT** per eligible user |
| **Merchant referral:** bring in a new user as a Merchant who completes their first eligible purchase | **2 USDT** per eligible user |

## The 30 USDT rule

To qualify, the new user's first purchase must be a single, successful HashLiquid **P2P Buy order of at least 30 USDT**.

* Both Maker Buy and Taker Buy orders can qualify.
* Orders can't be combined. For example, orders of 20 USDT and 15 USDT don't qualify.
* Sell orders, cancelled orders, and invalid orders don't count.

## One reward per verified person

* Rewards are tied to your verified identity (KYC), not to an account. Each person can receive the first-purchase reward only once.
* Accounts linked to the same verified identity are assessed together, so using another account or login method doesn't earn extra rewards.
* Each new user can have only one referral source: a user referral or a merchant referral, not both.
* Referral rewards are limited to 50 rewarded users per verified identity.

## When rewards are paid

Rewards are first recorded as **Credit**, then paid in USDT to your external wallet on **BNB Smart Chain (BEP20)** after review.

| Reward | Paid |
| --- | --- |
| **First purchase** | **Weekly.** Each week runs from Sunday 00:00 to Saturday 23:59:59 UTC. Approved rewards are paid on Tuesday, and payment results and TXIDs are recorded from Tuesday to Wednesday. |
| **User and merchant referrals** | **Monthly**, within the first 7 calendar days of the following month. |

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Save your receiving address in the Reward Center.** If no address is saved when the settlement list is generated, the affected reward expires and cannot be recovered.
{% endhint %}
