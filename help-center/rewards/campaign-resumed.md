# Notice: Resumption of the New User Trading Campaign

Dear HashLiquid Users,

We are happy to announce that the New User Trading Campaign (Trade, Invite and Earn) is back online, effective November 17, 2026. Thank you for your patience and support while our team completed the necessary reviews and verification.

Campaign rewards have been revised. The revised rewards apply from November 17, 2026.

## Revised campaign rewards

| Activity | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Complete your first eligible purchase | **1 USDT**, one time |
| Refer an eligible new trading user | **1 USDT** per user |
| Bring in an eligible new trading user as a Merchant | **2 USDT** per user |

**Please Note:**

1. To qualify, a new user must complete a single HashLiquid P2P Buy order of at least 30 USDT.
2. Rewards are assessed by verified identity. Each person can receive the first-purchase reward only once.
3. Rewards are first recorded as Credit and paid in USDT via BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) after review. First-purchase rewards are paid weekly, and referral rewards are paid monthly.
4. Make sure a valid USDT receiving address on BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) is saved in your Reward Center. If no address is saved when the settlement list is generated, the affected reward expires.
5. Activity completed before the pause will be assessed in accordance with the campaign [Terms and Conditions](user-rewards-guide.md#campaign-rules).

For full details, see [Trade, Invite and Earn](trade-invite-and-earn.md), the [User Rewards Guide](user-rewards-guide.md), and the [Merchant Referral Guide](merchant-referral-guide.md).
