# Trade, Invite and Earn

{% hint style="warning" %}
**This campaign is temporarily paused.** New participation is suspended while we complete reviews and verification. Regular P2P trading is not affected. [Read the announcement](campaign-pause.md)
{% endhint %}

Complete your first eligible purchase and receive **3 USDT**. Invite new users to earn referral rewards.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Campaign starts:** November 2, 2026. All campaign and settlement times are in UTC.
{% endhint %}

## Campaign rewards

| Activity | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Complete your first eligible purchase | **3 USDT**, one time |
| Refer an eligible new trading user | **3 USDT** per user |
| Bring in an eligible new trading user as a Merchant | **5 USDT** per user |

To qualify, the new user must complete a HashLiquid **P2P Buy order of at least 30 USDT in a single transaction**. User and merchant referral rewards are each limited to **50 rewarded users** per verified identity during the campaign.

All three rewards share a total allocation of **10,000 USDT**. Rewards are first recorded as **Credit** and, once approved, paid in USDT via **BSC (BEP20)**.

## How to participate

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
**Sign in and complete KYC**

Register or sign in to HashLiquid and complete identity verification.

Full guide: [How do I complete identity verification on HashLiquid?](../getting-started/identity-verification.md)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Complete your first purchase or invite new users**

Check your first-purchase task in the **Reward Center**. Once you complete KYC, you can access your referral link.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Check your wallet address and reward progress**

View or enter your external wallet address in the **Reward Center**, where you can also track your Credit, review status, and payments.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Check your receiving address promptly.** If no address is saved when the settlement list is generated, the affected reward expires and cannot be recovered.
{% endhint %}
