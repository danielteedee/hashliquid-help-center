# Notice: Temporary Pause of the New User Trading Campaign

Dear HashLiquid Users,

Thank you for the tremendous interest and participation in our New User Trading Campaign. We sincerely appreciate the support and enthusiasm shown by our growing community.

As part of our ongoing efforts to ensure the smooth operation and integrity of the campaign, HashLiquid has temporarily paused the New User Trading Campaign (Trade, Invite and Earn), effective November 3, 2026. This pause will allow our team to complete the necessary reviews and verification processes.

This temporary measure will enable us to review campaign trading activities, verify participant eligibility, and ensure that all rewards are distributed fairly and in accordance with the campaign rules.

**Please Note:**

1. New participation in the campaign is temporarily suspended until further notice.
2. Trading activities completed before the pause will be reviewed and verified.
3. Participants who completed the campaign requirements before the pause will have their activities assessed, and eligible rewards will be processed in accordance with the campaign [Terms and Conditions](user-rewards-guide.md#campaign-rules).
4. Users are encouraged to ensure that a valid USDT receiving address on BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) is saved in their Reward Center, in accordance with the campaign requirements.
5. We are working towards resuming the campaign within the next 24 hours, subject to the completion of the necessary reviews and verification processes. Any updates regarding the resumption will be communicated through our official channels.

Please note that this temporary pause applies specifically to the campaign and does not affect regular P2P trading activities on HashLiquid.
