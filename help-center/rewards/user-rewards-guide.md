# User Rewards Guide

{% hint style="warning" %}
**This campaign is temporarily paused.** New participation is suspended while we complete reviews and verification. Regular P2P trading is not affected. [Read the announcement](campaign-pause.md)
{% endhint %}

Complete your first eligible purchase to receive a one-time **3 USDT** reward. Once you complete KYC, earn **3 USDT** for each eligible new trading user you refer.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Campaign starts:** November 2, 2026. All campaign and settlement times are in UTC.
{% endhint %}

## Your rewards

| Reward | Requirement | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First Purchase** | Complete your first eligible purchase and pass review | 3 USDT, one time |
| **User Referral** | Refer a new user who completes an eligible purchase and passes review | 3 USDT per user |

Rewards are first recorded as **Credit**. Approved rewards are paid in USDT according to the [settlement schedule](#when-are-rewards-paid) below.

## First-purchase reward

### Who can participate

Complete registration or sign-in and KYC, then complete your first eligible purchase during the campaign and pass the eligibility review. Eligibility is assessed by verified identity, and each person can receive the new-user reward once.

Your account registration date alone does not determine eligibility. If you registered previously but have never traded on HashLiquid, you may still qualify. Eligible new users can participate whether they sign up directly or join through a user or merchant referral.

### What counts as an eligible purchase

* A successful HashLiquid **P2P Buy order of at least 30 USDT in a single transaction**.
* Both **Maker Buy** and **Taker Buy** orders can qualify.
* Multiple orders cannot be combined. For example, orders of 20 USDT and 15 USDT do not meet the single-order requirement.
* Cancelled or invalid orders do not count.
* Additional purchases do not create another new-user reward.

Go to **Reward Center** > **My Tasks** to check your progress. Once you complete an eligible purchase, **3 USDT** Credit is recorded.

New to buying? Follow the [Trading Tutorial](../p2p-trading/buy-crypto.md).

{% endhint %}

### What your friend needs to do

Your friend must:

1. Join through your valid referral link.
2. Complete registration or sign-in and KYC.
3. Complete their first eligible P2P Buy during the campaign.
4. Pass the review.

Clicking a link, registering, or completing KYC alone does not generate a referral reward.

### Reward and limit

You earn **3 USDT** for each eligible new user you refer, up to **50 rewarded users** per verified identity during this campaign.

| Approved users | Referral reward |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | 30 USDT |
| 30 | 90 USDT |
| 50 | 150 USDT |

The 51st and later referrals do not generate referral rewards. Eligible new users may still receive their own first-purchase reward, subject to the campaign period and remaining allocation. The limit applies to your verified identity and does not increase if you use another account or login method, or become a Merchant.

## Receiving your rewards

### Check your receiving address

Rewards are paid to an external wallet through **BSC (BEP20)**. Check your receiving address in the **Reward Center**:

* If the page asks you to enter an address, provide an external wallet address that supports USDT on BSC (BEP20).
* If an address is already displayed, check that it is correct.

A manually entered address must start with `0x` and contain 42 characters. Passing the format check does not verify that you own the address or that it can receive the reward.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Check your address as soon as you complete your task.** The address saved when the settlement list is generated is used for payment. If no address is saved at that time, the affected reward expires and cannot be recovered. An address added afterwards applies only to newly accumulated rewards.
{% endhint %}

### When are rewards paid

| Reward | Settlement | Payment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First purchase** | Weekly, from Sunday 00:00 UTC to Saturday 23:59:59 UTC | Scheduled for Tuesday. Payment results and TXIDs are recorded from Tuesday to Wednesday. |
| **User referral** | Monthly | Within the first 7 calendar days of the month after the campaign month. |

Rewards requiring further review are excluded from their scheduled payment batch and processed after review.

### Where to check your rewards

| Location | What you can view |
| --- | --- |
| **My Tasks** | First-purchase task and new-user reward progress |
| **My Referrals** | Referral link, user count, rewards, and X/50 progress |
| **Reward records / Payout History** | Reward status, paid amounts, and TXIDs |

{% hint style="info" %}
**What is Credit?** Credit is a recorded reward that has not been paid yet. It cannot be traded, transferred, or manually withdrawn. Once a reward is paid, the amount is removed from Credit and added to **Payout History**.
{% endhint %}

Reward records appear in the Reward Center, not in your asset transaction history or order history.

## Campaign rules

* **Shared allocation:** the new-user, user referral, and merchant referral rewards share a total allocation of **10,000 USDT**. Once the allocation is fully reserved, no new Credit is created. Credit already recorded remains subject to review and settlement.
* **One referral source per new user:** each new user can have only one eligible referral source and cannot generate both a user referral reward and a merchant referral reward.
* **One identity:** accounts linked to the same verified identity are assessed together.
* **Not eligible:** self-referrals, duplicate participation, fake users, circular referrals, artificial trading, and self-trading between linked accounts do not generate eligible rewards. If self-trading is detected, all outstanding rewards for that identity are suspended pending review.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>

<summary>Do I need to make a purchase before I can invite friends?</summary>

No. Your referral function unlocks once you complete KYC.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why have I not received a reward after sharing my link?</summary>

Your friend must join through the valid link, meet the new-user requirements, complete their first eligible purchase, and pass review. The referral limit and remaining campaign allocation also apply.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why can I see Credit but have not received USDT?</summary>

Credit is a recorded reward that has not been paid yet. First-purchase rewards are settled weekly, and referral rewards are settled monthly. Payment is subject to review.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I receive a missed reward after adding my wallet address?</summary>

No. If your address is missing when the settlement list is generated, the affected reward expires. An address added later applies only to newly accumulated rewards.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I need to complete KYC again if I sign in with Binance?</summary>

If you sign in with a Binance account and your verified account and completed KYC status are provided to HashLiquid, **Create Account** and **Complete KYC** appear as completed in **My Tasks**. You can then proceed to the first-purchase task.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Where will my rewards be sent?</summary>

Rewards are paid to an external wallet through BSC (BEP20).

* **Binance account sign-in:** enter your receiving wallet address in the Reward Center.
* **Supported CEX or DeFi wallet connection:** your connected wallet address is displayed automatically. Check that it is correct.

Make sure your receiving wallet supports USDT on BSC (BEP20).

</details>
