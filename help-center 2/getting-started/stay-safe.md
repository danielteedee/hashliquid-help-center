# How do I stay safe on HashLiquid?

Most problems on P2P platforms come from a few well-known tricks. Follow these rules on every order.

{% hint style="warning" %}
HashLiquid Support will never ask for your password, verification code, private key, or recovery phrase. Anyone who asks is not HashLiquid Support.
{% endhint %}

## Protect your account

* Never share your password, verification code, private key, or recovery phrase.
* Connecting a wallet never requires your recovery phrase or private key.
* When you sign in with Binance Login, approve the request only if you recognize HashLiquid and the Binance Account shown.
* Never give an online-banking password or card PIN to anyone, including when adding a Payment Method.

## Watch out for these scams

* **Fake payment proof.** A screenshot, text message, or payment notification from the buyer is not proof of payment. Check your own bank or payment account.
* **Moving the chat elsewhere.** Keep all order communication in the HashLiquid order chat. If the counterparty asks to continue on another app, stay in the order chat.
* **Payments from someone else.** If the payment comes from an account that doesn't match the order, don't release the crypto.
* **Pressure to hurry.** Never release crypto because the counterparty is pressuring you.
* **Fake support agents.** Real HashLiquid Support will never ask for your password, private key, or recovery phrase. Start support chats yourself from the headset icon on HashLiquid.

## When you buy

* Send the exact amount only to the account shown in the order.
* Select **I've Paid** only after the transfer has gone through.
* Don't cancel an order after you have paid, unless the funds have been returned to your payment account.

## When you sell

* Use only a payment account in your own name.
* Release crypto only after the full payment has arrived in your own account.
* If the amount is incomplete or the details don't match the order, don't release. Use the order chat, and raise a Dispute if needed.

## When you move crypto

On-chain transfers normally cannot be reversed. Before you approve a deposit or withdrawal, check the asset (**USDT**), the network (**BNB Smart Chain**), the amount, and the receiving account or wallet.

## If something goes wrong

1. Keep the order open and explain the problem in the order chat.
2. [Open a Dispute](../troubleshooting-and-support/disputes.md) if you can't resolve it with the counterparty.
3. [Contact HashLiquid Support](../troubleshooting-and-support/contact-hash-support.md) for account, deposit, or withdrawal problems.
