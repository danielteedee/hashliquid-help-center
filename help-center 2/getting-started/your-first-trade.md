# Your first trade on HashLiquid

{% hint style="success" %}
🎁 **Trade, Invite and Earn:** complete your first eligible purchase of at least 30 USDT in a single order to earn 3 USDT. [See the campaign](../rewards/trade-invite-and-earn.md)
{% endhint %}

New to HashLiquid? Follow these five steps to set up your account and complete your first buy order. Each step links to a full guide.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
**Sign in**

Access HashLiquid with email, Google, Binance Login, Binance Wallet, or WalletConnect.

Full guide: [How do I access HashLiquid?](access-hashliquid.md)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Verify your identity**

Open **Verifications** from your account menu. Have a valid ID Card or Passport ready and use a device with a working camera.

Full guide: [How do I complete identity verification on HashLiquid?](identity-verification.md)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Add a Payment Method**

Add a bank or payment account in your own name. Buyers pay you through it when you sell, and the account-holder name must match your verified identity.

Full guide: [How do I add, edit, or remove a Payment Method?](payment-methods.md)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Place a buy order**

Select **Express** > **Buy**, enter an amount, choose how you'll pay, and select **Select Ads**. Review the price, limits, and **Terms & Remarks**, then select **Place Order**.

Full guide: [How do I buy crypto on HashLiquid?](../p2p-trading/buy-crypto.md)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Pay and wait for release**

Transfer the exact amount to the account shown in the order, then select **I've Paid** before the countdown ends. The seller releases the crypto after confirming your payment.

Full guide: [How do I buy crypto on HashLiquid?](../p2p-trading/buy-crypto.md)
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Before your first trade, take two minutes to read [How do I stay safe on HashLiquid?](stay-safe.md)
{% endhint %}
